# 📂 Dataset Description
The dataset contains four worksheets:

- `customer_dim`
- `sales_dim`
- `product_dim`
- `sales_fact`

The dataset includes **transaction-level e-commerce sales data** with the following fields:

Sales ID, Order Date, Customer ID, Gender, Loyalty Level, Age, State, Product ID, Store ID, Quantity, Unit Price, Discount, Payment Type, and Total Amount.

# 📈 Key Insights
🧍 Demographic Performance
Middle‑aged customers dominate sales, contributing nearly 47% of total revenue.

Young buyers account for only 23%, suggesting potential for targeted marketing campaigns.

Older customers remain steady contributors but show limited growth.

# 🌍 Regional Trends
North and East regions lead with over 58% combined share of total sales.

West region underperforms (≈18%), indicating room for expansion or improved distribution.

Regional imbalance highlights where promotional focus could yield higher returns.

# 🛍️ Product Category Insights
Sports and Home categories are top performers, each contributing around 24% of total sales.

Beauty and Clothing trail behind, suggesting opportunities for product diversification or bundling strategies.

Electronics maintain moderate but consistent sales, reflecting stable demand.

# 💻 Channel Analysis
Online sales lead with 42% share, slightly ahead of Flagship stores (39%).

Outlet sales contribute 19%, showing potential for optimization through targeted promotions.

The digital channel’s dominance underscores the importance of e‑commerce investment.
